# CIC-Transfer-Pathways-Summer-Research-Project

The following project is a Data-Science based research conducted specifically on the how have rising temperatures and CO₂ emissions negatively impacted forest ecosystems, and how can these changes be tracked over time? In our research, we aim to explore the relationship between emissions and forest health and compare data across multiple countries to understand long-term trends. By analyzing data from three comprehensive datasets from Kaggle (an online resource used to find datasets on various topics): CO₂ and greenhouse gas emissions¹, global surface temperature changes², and forest area coverage worldwide³. We seek to uncover patterns that illustrate the environmental consequences of these changes.
